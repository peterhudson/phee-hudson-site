New this season

Phee has spent a lifetime climbing, ski-touring and paddling through the landscapes she paints. The latest canvases return to the Rockies in winter and travel north to the great peaks of the St Elias range: Mt Logan, Mt St Elias and Denali.

Each one starts with a sketch or photograph made on the trail and is finished in the boathouse studio, in the bold, saturated acrylic colour her collectors know her for.

About Phee

Born in Rhodesia (now Zimbabwe) and trained at the University of Cape Town, Phee came to Canada in the late 1970s and has lived on Vancouver Island since 1981. A mountaineer, backcountry skier and sea kayaker, she turned to painting full time in 2006 and became a Senior Member of the Federation of Canadian Artists in 2019. Her paintings hang in collections on four continents.

She is also the author of field guides to the coastal flowers, alpine flowers and trees of the Pacific Northwest.
