About the artist

Phee Hudson

A mountaineer, backcountry skier and sea kayaker who paints the places she has been.

Born in Rhodesia (now Zimbabwe), Phee earned an arts degree at the University of Cape Town and worked in England before coming to Canada in the late 1970s. She has lived on Vancouver Island since 1981 and paints from a boathouse studio on the water.

After many years running Mineral World and the Scratch Patch in Sidney, she turned to painting full time in 2006, first in watercolour and now mainly in acrylic. She became a Senior Member of the Federation of Canadian Artists in 2019, has won numerous awards, and her paintings hang in collections on four continents.

She is also the author of field guides to the coastal flowers, alpine flowers and trees of the Pacific Northwest.
