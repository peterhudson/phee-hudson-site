Three ideas for a new website

Each of these uses your own paintings and words from phillipahudson.com, presented in a different style. Click into each one, try it on your phone too, and tell Peter which you like best, or which bits of each.

Dark · cinematic
Concept 1

Summit

Full-screen paintings fade one into the next as you arrive. Dark, gallery-at-night feel that makes the colour glow. Everything on one scrolling page.
Light · classic
Concept 2

Gallery

A quiet, white-walled gallery. Every painting gets its own page with sizes, print prices and an “Enquire” button, so you can send people a link to one painting.
Warm · storytelling
Concept 3

Boathouse

Magazine-style, with a cover story for new work. Paintings are grouped by place (Rockies, Coast Mountains, the Island, the North, the sea) for browsing.

These are mock-ups. The forms and sign-ups don't send anything yet, and the paintings are shown straight from your current website. Nothing on your real site has been changed.
